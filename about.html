Our History

From Thomson Plaza to Today

2012

Thomson Plaza

Voilà Design Pte Ltd started in 2012 at Thomson Plaza with a simple ambition: to create beautiful, practical homes while building genuine relationships with the people we work with.

2013

HDB Licensed Contractor

In 2013, we became an HDB Licensed Contractor, and we have proudly maintained our licence ever since.

Growth

Goldhill Plaza & United Square

Over the years, Voilà grew from a small design practice into a team of around 30 designers and backend staff, with offices at Goldhill Plaza and United Square along the way.

1,000+

Projects Completed

Throughout our journey, we have completed more than 1,000 projects — from HDB homes and condominiums to landed properties, retail spaces and offices.

Every project has taught us something new. More importantly, every client has been part of our story.

A New Chapter

A Different Kind of Interior Design Company

As the years went by, we realised that bigger wasn’t necessarily better.

We wanted to return to what mattered most to us — being personally involved with our clients and their homes.

Family-Oriented

Hands-On by Choice

So, rather than continuing to grow purely in size, we made a conscious decision to restructure Voilà into a more family-oriented and hands-on company.

Today

Our Home at Tagore Lane

We even invested in our own office at Tagore Lane, creating a permanent home for Voilà and a comfortable space where our clients can meet our team, discuss their ideas and see the materials and finishes we work with.

The FamilyBehind Voilà

Design Director

On time andwithin budget

Voilà offers a complete interior design solution from conceptualisation to execution, assuring successful completion on time and within budget.

01

Consultation

From the onset, we conduct a comprehensive audit first on the way you live and work, then proceed to planning the space, both from macro and micro perspectives. Our aim is always to ensure that the space is crafted specifically for you and your needs.

02

Design

Then we proceed to conceptualise the final vision, well-aware of your budget and timeline as well as goals. At this stage, we relook at various options and plausible solutions, to achieve the most optimal solution. Our desire is to work closely together with you, capturing your vision fully.

03

Execution

Finally, we execute the vision by managing the entire supply chain as well as the multitude of contractors for you. We ensure the project is on budget, on time and of the best quality and finishing.

04

Support

At the end of the road, the final result is an impression that not only lasts a lifetime but leaves you always astounded at our magical touch. Our after-sales service gives you that necessary peace of mind.
