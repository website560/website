# Voilà website admin: how to use it

**Where:** https://voila.sg/admin (the page is not linked from the website and is hidden from Google)
**Login:** marketings@voila.sg and the password you were given. Change it any time in **Settings**.

Changes go live on the website the moment you save. Refresh the website to see them.

## What you can manage

**Portfolio**
- **Projects:** the homes in the portfolio. Add a project with its style, location, property type and the YouTube home tour. Drop in photos; the first one is the cover. Drag photos to reorder, "Make cover", or remove. Each project gets its own page automatically, and its photos join the gallery.
- **Home tour films:** paste a YouTube link and the thumbnail is fetched. Set the style, location and length.
- **Extra gallery photos:** loose photos for the gallery that don't belong to a project.

**About page**
- **Google reviews:** name, stars, date and the review text. Shown on the home page wall and in the testimonial loop.
- **Team:** name, role, portrait photo and write-up.
- **Our history:** the timeline milestones, oldest first.
- **As seen on:** the press logos (PNG with transparent background, or SVG).
- **Instagram:** the posts in the Instagram strip. Paste the post link and upload its image.

**Site**
- **Site text:** the big headlines, the intro tagline, the closing band, the Google rating numbers, contact details and opening hours.
- **Settings:** change your password.

## The basics
- **Add** with the orange button at the top right of a section.
- **Edit** by clicking a row. **Delete** with "Delete" on the row (it asks first).
- **Reorder** by dragging the dotted handle on the left of a row. The order in the panel is the order on the website.
- **Search** the box at the top of each list to find something quickly.
- **Photos:** drag them straight into the upload box or click to choose. Large phone photos are shrunk automatically, so there's no need to resize first. Portrait photos work best for the team; landscape for projects and history.
- **Undo:** every section has "Previous versions". Each save is kept, so if something is deleted by mistake, restore the version from before.

## Good to know
- The admin works on a phone too, but photo-heavy work is easier on a computer.
- Keep the Google reviews word for word as they appear on Google.
- The home page hero film and the logo are not editable here; ask your web developer to change those.
